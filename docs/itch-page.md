Shift the land of Anatolia. Bring water and roads to every village while the

## Page body

**Fay Hattı** ("fault line") is a daily sliding-tile puzzle made for GitHub
Game Off 2026 (theme: SHIFT).

Slide rows and columns of a 5×5 parchment map. Tiles wrap around the edge
and glide past the villages, mountains, the spring and the caravanserai,
which never move. Lead the river from the spring and the road from the
caravanserai to every village that needs them.

After each move a small earthquake shifts one row on its own. The red band
shows which row is next. Solve the map with your move and the ground stays
calm.

- Same daily map for everyone, plus an endless journey in three difficulty
  tiers (Easy, Normal, Hard)
- Bridges, ferries, caravan stops, ancient ruins and double-fault quakes in
  endless mode
- 2-second optimal hint glow, undo that rewinds the quakes too, a live
  "villages linked" counter
- Share a 640×640 result card without spoilers
- English and Turkish, touch, mouse and keyboard, high contrast, no install

One HTML file, all graphics drawn in code and all sound synthesized at
runtime. No ads, no tracking servers, no network requests.

Source (MIT): <https://github.com/Furkiozknn/fay-hatti>
